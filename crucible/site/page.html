Crucible

Real work in. Honest, trainable RL environments out.

Every day, teams already produce expert judgement: reviews, corrections, test results, approvals, outcomes. Crucible turns those traces into live environments with dense step-level rewards, tasks that frontier models fail, and benchmarks that score every step.

Foundation

A typed core, live worlds, and people who only say what is on record

M0 Core contracts

One typed language

Every module speaks it; nothing malformed gets in.

Drag to turn

Worlds, traces, signals, milestones, rewards, tasks and runs are versioned documents, serialized as canonical JSON and content-hashed. The core holds no domain logic.

  • Strict schemas. Unknown keys rejected, wrong versions refused.
  • Content-addressed store. Corruption is detected on read.
  • Provenance everywhere. Every artifact names what it came from.
  • Plugin registry. Packs register themselves; the core never imports them.

M1 World runtime

Live worlds, rewound exactly

Apps with state, actors, a virtual clock, and exact snapshots.

Drag to turn

A spec becomes a running world: databases, documents, inboxes, workspaces and GPUs as apps, people as actors, events on a virtual clock. Policies step it; graders read it through a view the policy never sees.

$ crucible world validate world.yaml --gold actions.json
  • One choke point. Every tool call is validated, permissioned and bounded.
  • Exact restore. Fingerprints of every app prove the rewind.
  • Hardened sandbox. Demoted user, no way out to the network.
  • Virtual clock. Replies and rule changes fire on world time, so runs replay exactly.
15 / 15GPU restores bit-identical
5 / 5seed worlds, 3 of 3 runs on GPU
< 5 msstep overhead
M2 Grounded actors

People who answer from the record

Customers, reviewers and approvers, simulated without invention.

Drag to turn

Everyone who is not the policy lives inside the world. Grounded actors reply only with what real people actually wrote, and say “I have no record of that” otherwise.

  • Quote mode. The model selects; the record speaks.
  • No future leaks. Records stay hidden until their timestamp.
  • Citations checked. Numbers and dates must match exactly.
  • Real reviewers. Recorded comments replay at matching code.

M3 Trace connectors

Capture real work, redacted first

Many sources in, one trace format out.

Ticket and ERP exports, audit logs, telemetry, live model traffic, code history, test reports and policy documents become one typed trace. Redaction runs before anything is stored.

  • Idempotent. Re-ingest returns identical content hashes.
  • Probes tagged. Answer-key lookups are marked, never scored.

M4 Expert signals

Judgement, already in the logs

Corrections, approvals and reverts become graded evidence.

Drag to turn

Signals are typed, pinned to the step they grade (the event that last wrote the object), and weighed by how well they predicted what happened next.

  • Credit follows writes. Last-writer attribution.
  • Learned reliability. Weak signals are down-weighted automatically.
  • Pivots. A corrected mistake becomes a wrong/fixed pair.

Reward

Step-level credit that cannot be gamed, decided by graders the agent cannot touch

M6 Dense credit

Reward at every step

Progress you can measure, and cannot farm.

Drag to turn

Progress Φ is the weighted share of milestones met, each counting only once its prerequisites hold. Each step earns ΔΦ, so the best policy never changes; gates and a capped partial term keep anything short of success below success.

reward  R = G · [ O·(1 + αp + βe) + κ·(1 − O)·Φ ]    κ ≤ 0.3
  • Potential shaping. ΔΦ telescopes; loops net zero.
  • Gates first. One hard failure means zero.
  • Capped partial credit. Always below finishing.
  • Branch values. Estimated from restored snapshots.
6 / 6reward-safety properties
0.72Φ AUC vs 0.67 judge
Φ = 1oracle; base scores 0
M7 Verifier kit

Graders behind glass

Hidden, isolated, and tested against every cheat.

Drag to turn

Graders run in a separate, network-less container and trust only what the candidate cannot write: artifacts compared against hidden references, never exit codes or printed claims.

  • Untouchable channels. Only evidence the candidate cannot forge.
  • Differential checks. Against hidden references and fresh draws.
  • Guarded performance. Noisy runs are masked, never failed.
  • Attack suite. With controls proving each defence matters.

M8 Forge

Frontier-hard, by construction

A swarm of roles behind information walls.

Drag to turn

Specialist roles turn traces into new worlds and tasks. The solution and the hidden checks exist before any solver tries, and each role sees only the inputs it is allowed to see.

  • Canary breach. A leaked oracle marker fails the leaker.
  • Traps from real failures. Each cites an expert signal.
  • Learnable band. Training pass rate aimed at 0.1 to 0.7.

M9 Acceptance contract

Evidence, or it does not ship

Eighteen executable checks between a task and the pool.

Drag to turn

The contract proves the oracle passes, floors fail, checks kill mutants, nothing leaks and the grader resists attacks. No model decides a gate, and every defect ever found becomes a new detector.

$ crucible accept toy

  • Mutation score ≥ 0.9. Checks must kill generated defects.
  • Content-addressed. An edit re-grades only what changed.
  • Growing registry. New detectors re-check every task.

M10 Runtime

Infrastructure is never failure

k samples, any policy, every failure labelled.

Drag to turn

Runs tasks × policies × harnesses with budgets, retries and resumable journals. Infrastructure faults are masked out of every average; policy faults are scored.

  • Blame on every failure. Infra masked, policy scored.
  • Reserve, then settle. Crash-safe spend accounting.
  • Resume anywhere. Finished work never re-dispatches.
  • Regrade from artifacts. No agent re-run needed.

M12 Training

Asynchronous RL on exact tokens

Rollouts and updates overlap; staleness stays bounded.

Drag to turn

Rollouts run against live worlds while weights update. Every token carries its policy version, and staleness is enforced at admission, at batch selection and in the loss.

$ crucible train job.toml
GRPOgroup advantagesGAEwith segmentsOPDon-policy distillationGARmass-conserving

  • Three topologies. Colocated sync, partial, disaggregated.
  • Off-policy corrections. Token-level importance weights, clipped and masked.
  • Dense turn credit. Mapped onto each call’s tokens.

LH Long horizons

A million tokens, honestly

Compact, checkpoint, crash, resume, and still add up.

Drag to turn

One episode can run thousands of steps. Near its budget the policy writes its own summary, a trained call that opens a new segment; the world checkpoints every K steps, so a crash resumes mid-episode.

  • Self-written compaction. At 80% of the budget.
  • Segments. Each compaction opens a fresh, trained sequence.
  • Exact telescoping. ΣΔΦ equals the final reward.
  • Linear storage. Tokens kept as deltas.

RLM Recursive agent

Context lives in a kernel

A short prompt over a persistent Python memory.

Drag to turn

The task, tools, every observation and every message are variables in a sandboxed kernel the model reads by writing code. It fans out sub-calls, and old turns are archived verbatim where it can still search them.

  • Prompt as variables. TASK, TOOLS, OBS, INBOX, HISTORY.
  • One step per call. Each world call is one world step.
  • Trainable tree. Sub-calls share their parent’s credit.
  • Exact restore. Journal replay, digest-checked.

RLM Task manager

Tasks that repair themselves

Plan, implement, review, verify, and only then commit.

Drag to turn

The same recursive core runs a loop that repairs and improves tasks. A different model reviews every change, and an objective verifier, never a model, has the final word.

  • Adversarial review. A second model hunts for flaws.
  • Objective verifier. Acceptance checks, not opinions.
  • Audit floor. A candidate can only strengthen its checks.
  • Journaled. A crashed loop resumes where it stopped.

M13 Benchmarks

Where agents fail, step by step

Progress, not just pass or fail.

Drag to turn

Benchmarks measure gate profiles, how far and how fast agents get, regressions and cost. Fixed anchors plus freshly generated items share one IRT scale, so versions stay comparable and resist contamination.

  • Paired comparison. Exact McNemar and paired bootstrap.

M11 Export

One episode, one reward

Every episode replays into the same reward and step files.

Drag to turn

An episode is normalized into canonical actions and replayed in a fresh world. One reward producer grades the replay, so its reward and step files come out byte-identical every time, ready for training or for audit.

$ crucible export --to canonical --pack finance_ap
  • Canonical actions. Call ids derived, never trusted from the transcript.
  • Fresh replay. Actions rerun against a new world, not a saved answer.
  • Byte-identical. The same reward file on every replay.
  • Masks survive. A masked grade is never written as zero.

M14 Packs

Domains plug in

One manifest per domain; the core stays generic.

Drag to turn

A pack holds everything domain-specific: sources, credit rules, generators, policies and fixtures. Each one is checked at three levels before anything uses it.

Quickstart

        
No model, no GPU, no API key.

        
The whole path runs on a laptop CPU in a couple of minutes. Every command prints the next one.

      

      
# a domain pack, checked at every level
$ crucible pack new demo --dir ./demo
$ crucible pack check ./demo --level semantic

# label: logs → traces with dense rewards + datasets
$ crucible label --pack toy --out label

# build: logs → accepted tasks → benchmark report
$ crucible build toy --out build

# a real domain: accounts payable
$ crucible bench run finance_ap --out runs
