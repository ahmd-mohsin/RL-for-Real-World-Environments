# M2 study: simulated users and people, Crucible vs reference frameworks (2026-10-12)

Scope: `src/crucible/actors/{simulated,grounded,records,grounding,scripted,fidelity,base}.py` at HEAD `3d629a1`, compared with
the local references under `RL-Environments/06-frameworks-and-env-code/` plus MiMo (`xiaomimimo-verl/third_party/mimoagent-osr`).
Paths below are relative to those roots. `CTU` = `nvidia-nemo-gym/resources_servers/conversational_tool_use_simulation/app.py`.

Caveats:
- The working tree has **uncommitted edits to `grounding.py` and `records.py`** from the parallel M2 grounding audit. They fix
  number-substring matching, "Sure, ..." prefixes, negation flips, untimed records leaking into replays, and ASCII-only tokens.
  I ran read-only probes against the working tree: "5 days" vs "15 days", "was not approved" and "Great, the invoice was
  approved" are now rejected. Those defects are left out of section 2. `simulated.py`, `grounded.py`, `fidelity.py` and
  `llm.py` are unchanged.
- tau2-bench upstream is **not vendored** in any local reference. It is installed at runtime (NeMo-Gym `responses_api_agents/tau2/README.md`;
  `tau_common/taskset.py:55-56` pins `sierra-research/tau2-bench@b7ea907`). tau2 behaviour is therefore cited through
  its vendored copies: NeMo-Gym `indian_banking` (the guidelines file is "a lightly edited copy of tau2-bench's
  `simulation_guidelines.md`") and the harbor tau3 runtime server, which drives `tau2.user.UserSimulator` directly.

## 4. Where Crucible is stronger

- **Online consistency judge with regeneration.** The policy only ever sees replies that passed the judge
  (simulated.py:143-171). CTU judges after the fact, and the others do not judge the user at all.
- **Masking discipline.** Sim and provider failures are masked by kind and blame (base.py:5-6; run.py:57-66; policy.py:556-577).
  AReaL (agent.py:237-244), indian_banking (README:129, app.py:238-243) and harbor (evaluate.py:212-226) score a sim
  failure as 0, which teaches the policy from infrastructure noise.
- **Record-grounded people.** `GroundedResponder` offers verbatim, quote and llm modes, the time-visibility rule, citations kept out of the policy's view,
  and an entailment audit (grounded.py, records.py, fidelity.py). No reference grounds a simulated person in recorded history.
  The measured M2 gate results (0/225 fabricated sentences in quote mode) have no counterpart in the references.
- **Multi-party worlds.** Simulated, grounded, scripted (`RelayActor`, `CheckRunnerActor` reporting only pass/fail through the verifier
  view) and human or A2A actors share one World. Each actor's memory is snapshotted in the WorldRef (base.py:31-37). Every reference is two-party
  (user and agent).
- **Replies to the policy are leak-scrubbed** (world.py:765-770). The **budget is hard and there is a record/replay cache** (llm.py:181-194).
  **Prefill with a `continuation_start` marker** matches CTU's `prefill_message_count`.
- **Early DONE is regenerated rather than failing the sample** (simulated.py:157-158). CTU only records it as a failure (2161-2169).
