## 4. Where Crucible is stronger

- **Hard dollar budget with masking.** `BudgetExceeded` and provider 402 / `insufficient_quota` both become `crucible:credits_exhausted`, which is masked (`llm.py:195-196, 225-226`). Only mini-swe-agent has a dollar cap, and its exhaustion is a scored exit. `usage_blank` keeps missing usage from going unnoticed (`llm.py:241-248`); Harbor books $0 silently (`HB/llms/lite_llm.py:554-566`).
- **Record and replay of model calls**, keyed by sample (once D1 is fixed). No reference caches model calls; verifiers only replays idempotent SDK retries.
- **Multi-actor ordering.** Inbox messages are held until a parallel batch is complete (`policy.py:336-338`), which keeps OpenAI-style histories valid in multi-actor Worlds. No reference has other actors writing into the policy's context mid-batch.
- **Masking taxonomy.** Every failure carries its own mask and blame (`policy.py:572-577`, `C/core/schema/run.py:56-64`). A policy's breach of the verification boundary is scored (`policy.py:544-546`). An exposed answer is masked (`:548-550`). References mostly mask nothing (verl, SkyRL) or send failures to a side file (NeMo-Gym).
- **Context strategies.** Five compaction strategies, plus compaction as a policy action, with segments recorded as trained calls (`policy.py:80-101, 249-281, 388-400`). Only verifiers and Harbor summarize; neither records segments for training.
- **Token capture.** Linear delta compression across turns (`policy.py:195-209`) and a `policy_version` stamp on each call (`llm.py:139, 291`).
- **Game seats.** SeatView and MoveLog (`C/actors/seat_view.py`) give each seat the other seats' moves and its legal actions, plus turn nudges. No reference has turn-based multi-seat loops.
- **Snapshots and checkpoints.** A fingerprint-deduplicated snapshot series (`policy.py:485-488`) and a policy `state_dict` that resumes mid-episode (`:283-301`).
- **More lenient argument parsing than verifiers, NeMo-Gym and OpenEnv.** Double-encoded JSON is accepted, and a non-object is answered with a typed error rather than a crash.
