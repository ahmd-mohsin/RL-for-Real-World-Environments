# M6 credit, M7 verify, M8 forge: what other frameworks do better, ranked fixes (2026-10-11)

Crucible already covers most of what the frameworks offer: prewrite-0, two-sandbox grading, F2P / exact-count tests,
OpenEnv RFC 008's oracle/floor/canary checks, learnability with Wilson bands, a solver ladder, lineage sealing, and an
attack suite with controls. The gaps below were confirmed in the code.

## Structural violations (fix first)

**V1. A candidate can turn a perf failure into a masked grade.**
- `verify/runtime/patterns/perf.py:141-142` returns `infra` whenever a failing attempt is contaminated, including when
  only the *candidate's* rounds are noisy.
- So a slow candidate with jittery timing is masked instead of scored 0, and gives no negative gradient.
- Fix: mask only for original-side contamination or a busy guard sample.

**V2. Other masks the candidate can trigger.**
- `kill_cohort` raises `InfraError` when candidate processes survive (`trusted.py:157-160`).
- A hung CUDA kernel stuck in uninterruptible sleep would also be masked.
- The only guard is a global masked-share breaker (`train/runtime.py:286-291`). There is no accounting per task or per
  checkpoint, and no record of who caused a mask.

**V3. Dense credit ignores gates and confirmed hacks.**
- `compose.turn_advantages` has no gate or hack field and computes ΔΦ terms for every live episode.
- `steps.to_turn_shaping` (`steps.py:41-43`) exports raw ΔΦ the same way.
- So a gate-violating or hack-zeroed episode still gives its turns positive credit.
- With sd = 0, `scale` falls back to 1/spread, so the model learns from pure process reward. SmolDataEnvs saw this
  exploited within 70 steps (`adithya-s-k-fineenvs/04-smoldataenvs/README.md:140-146`).

**V4. LLM judges read agent text unfenced.**
- `rubric_item.py:23-33` drops `{evidence}` straight into the prompt, and `parse_verdict` takes the last `VERDICT:`
  line.
- `hack_detector.py:61-73` feeds agent-derived evidence to the precision judge.
- The GAR quality judge moves up to λ of the passing advantage mass.

**V5. The bound in `compose.py` is not enforced.** The docstring says the local term never exceeds the outcome advantage,
but there is no clamp. Group centring also makes longer episodes earn more when the group's mean ΔΦ is negative.
